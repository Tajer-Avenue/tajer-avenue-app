# Tajer Avenue

Tajer Avenue is a UAE-focused multi-vendor marketplace for product businesses, service businesses, suppliers, sellers, and buyers.

## Product vision

A modern digital mall where every merchant gets a customizable storefront while customers can discover, shop, book services, and connect with businesses across the UAE.

## Planned customer experience

- Home / discovery
- New Drops, Trending, and Nearby
- Categories
- Stores
- Store profiles
- Products and services
- Product details
- Search
- Favorites
- Shared cart and checkout
- Account

## Planned merchant experience

- Seller dashboard
- My Store
- Store customization (logo, cover, colors, video, links, and theme)
- Products and services
- Orders
- Analytics
- Subscription

## Stack

- Flutter
- Supabase
